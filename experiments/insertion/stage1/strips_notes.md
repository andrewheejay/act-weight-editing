# Frame strips, what I saw

40 strips (30 failures, 10 strict successes; picks fixed by `strips_ins.py` with rng 0, every trajectory hash equal to design_default). Each strip is 10 frames, 44 steps apart (steps 0 to 396), 200 by 150 pixels per frame. All 30 failure strips were looked at on 2026-11-25 (10 per contact sheet, frames shrunk to about 160 by 120 pixels), and the 10 successes and 7 of the 13 class 3 strips were also viewed at full size in session 19. These are impressions at low resolution and none of the numbers in diagnosis.md rests on them. I could not see the tip relative to the socket opening in any frame.

- Class 3 (13 strips: 20019, 20037, 20090, 20158, 20251, 20302, 20497, 20499, 20620, 20647, 20814, 20832, 20912): in all of them both grippers are closed on their objects and the two objects are near each other in the last 3 to 4 frames (from about step 264). I cannot say from these images whether the peg is inside the socket.
- Class 4a (20276, 20578, 20605, 20794): the same picture.
- Class 2 (20788), class 1 (20024, 20192, 20540, 20688, 20710, 20821, 20825) and class 0 (20001, 20023, 20384, 20393, 20473): the contact sheets do not resolve which gripper holds which object. Nothing is claimed about these from the strips. The classes come from the recorded flags (DEVIATIONS.md, 2026-11-25).
- Sessions: the 10 strict successes and 7 class 3 strips were looked at at full size in session 19 (notes in PROGRESS.md), all 30 failure strips as contact sheets in session 20.
